# The De/Dey Family of Bengal — Research Archive

**A comprehensive genealogical, genetic, and historical research project on the De/Dey (দে/দেয়) family of Bengal**

*Research by Robin Dey | AI research assistance by the DEY-BENGAL system (Claude-based, Anthropic)*

---

## Overview

This repository contains the full research archive of the **DEY-BENGAL** project — a multi-year interdisciplinary study tracing the origins, social identity, and documented history of the De/Dey (also: Deb, De) family of Bengal, with particular focus on the eastern Bengal (Mymensingh) lineage.

The research spans:
- **Deep ancestry** (65,000 BCE to present) through population genetics
- **Medieval epigraphy** — copper plates of the Deva dynasty (1231–1243 CE)
- **Bengali kulagrantha** (genealogical texts) — systematic analysis of 5 volumes of Nagendranath Basu's *Banger Jatiya Itihas* (1913–1937)
- **Colonial documentation** — census records, district gazetteers, judicial archives
- **Micro-history** — the Mymensingh umbrella manufacturer Aswini Kumar De and his 1937 Calcutta High Court case (AIR 1938 Cal 216)

## Primary Sources Analysed

### Nagendranath Basu — *Banger Jatiya Itihas* (5 volumes)

| Volume | Year | Archive ID | Pages | Key Finding for De/Dey |
|--------|------|------------|-------|------------------------|
| Baishya Kanda | 1913 | in.ernet.dli.2015.340039 | 3,334 lines | **De/Dey ABSENT** → confirms Kayastha, not Vaishya |
| Rajanya Kanda | 1914 | in.ernet.dli.2015.355890 | 21,019 lines | 27 Bangaja families; Dey = #19; Shandilya gotra kulanayak |
| Uttarrariya Kayastha-kanda | 1929 | in.ernet.dli.2015.356667 | 2,321 lines | Deva absent → confirms Bangaja (East Bengal) identity |
| **Dakshin Rariya Kayastha-kanda** | **1933** | in.ernet.dli.2015.339587 | 4,536 lines | **10 gotras; Deva = #1 Siddha Maulika; 14 samajes** |
| Kayastha Kanda | 1937 | in.ernet.dli.2015.353523 | ~3,000 lines | Fn.183: Two Danujamardanas; Mymensingh manuscript |

All volumes available at [archive.org](https://archive.org)

### Other Key Primary Sources
- **Damodaradeva copper plates** (1234, 1236, 1243 CE) — earliest epigraphic evidence
- **Batu Bhatta, *Deva Bangsha*** — Shandilya lineage genealogy (c. 1300 CE; 18th-c. copy)
- **Dwija Bachaspati, *Bangaja Kulaji Sara Sangraha*** — 27-family Sanskrit verse
- **Hari Misra, *Karika Purana*** (c. 1265–1285 CE) — Kayastha kulina text
- ***Loke Nath Sen v. Aswini Kumar Dey***, AIR 1938 Cal 216 — Calcutta High Court judgment

## Genetic Evidence Summary

Source: **Pagani, L., Bose, S., Ayub, Q. and Tyler-Smith, C. (2017)**, "Kayasthas of Bengal: Legends, Genealogies, and Genetics," *Economic and Political Weekly*, 52(47): 44–53.

- **Y-DNA (Sample A, Dey family)**: H1a* — ancient indigenous South Asian paternal lineage; NOT steppe-derived
- **mtDNA**: M2 — ancient South Asian maternal haplogroup
- **Autosomal**: South Indian affinity (Sakilli-Kerala: 0.063 distance) — NOT North Indian/Kannauj
- **Interpretation**: Consistent with *maulika* classification (indigenous Bengal stock, not Kannauj migrant)
- **Limitation**: n=1; full population study of Bengali Dey families urgently needed

## The Mymensingh Connection

**Aswini Kumar De** (c. 1880–post-1937), Robin Dey's grandfather (Robin is his grandson):
- Umbrella manufacturer, **Mymensingh Burra Bazar**
- Brand: **"Aswini Chhata"** (swan/*haṃsa* trademark)
- Trademark: **Registry No. 964**, registered **23 August 1926**
- Court case: ***Loke Nath Sen v. Aswini Kumar Dey***, AIR 1938 Cal 216 (1 Sep 1937)
- Business established c. 1912; still operating at time of judgment (25+ years)

**Gobindachandra Deb Roy** — contemporary, western Mymensingh; High Court advocate; sent the Deva dynasty kulagrantha (Batu Bhatta manuscript) to Nagendranath Basu c. 1910–1914. Same community, same district, same era.
