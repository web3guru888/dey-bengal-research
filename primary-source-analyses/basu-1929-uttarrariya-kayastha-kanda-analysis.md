## Key Finding: Deva Family NOT in North Radhi System

The Uttarrariya (North Radhi) Kayastha Kanda Vol.3 covers **seven specific North Radhi Kayastha families**:
1. Vishvamitra gotra **Mitra** dynasty
2. Kashyapa gotra **Datta** dynasty
3. **Shandilya gotra Ghosh** dynasty
4. Kashyapa gotra **Das** dynasty
5. Bharadvaja gotra **Singha** dynasty
6. Bharadvaja gotra **Das** dynasty (2nd branch)
7. Moudgalya gotra **Kar** dynasty

**The Deva dynasty is NOT covered** in this volume. This confirms that:
- The Deva/De/Dey family is a **Bangaja** (East Bengal) family, not a North Radhi family
- The Bangaja system (centered at Chandradwip/Barisal) was distinct from the North Radhi system

## Notable Finding: Danujamardana and Raja Ganesh (North Radhi Perspective)

Line 1178:
```
রাজা গণেশ ও রাজা দনুজমর্দন দেবকে অভিন্ন বলিয়া মনে করা যাইতে পারে।
```
**Translation**: "It can be considered that Raja Ganesh and Raja Danujamardana Dev were the same person."

**NOTE**: This is from the earlier Uttarrariya Kayastha-kanda (1929), before Basu fully corrected the conflation in 1933 and 1937. In Rajanya Kanda (1914) he had confused TWO Danujamardanas (13th c. Bikrampur and 15th c. Chandradwip). Here in 1929, he seems to be conflating Danujamardana Dev (coins 1417 CE, Chandradwip) with **Raja Ganesh** (c. 1414-1418 CE, Muslim ruler of Bengal).

**The research consensus** (Rajanya Kanda Footnote 183, confirmed in 1933 and 1937): These are TWO DIFFERENT persons from TWO DIFFERENT periods.

Line 1584 also mentions "Danujamardana" in connection with Dinajpur ("danujamardan-pur" = Dinajpur). This is a separate confusion.

## Shandilya Gotra Ghosh Dynasty (Comparison Case)

Lines 1828, 1835, 2267: "শাণ্ডিল্য গোত্র ঘোষ বংশ" — The North Radhi Ghosh dynasty has **Shandilya gotra**.

**Comparison with Deva dynasty**: 
- Deva (Bangaja) Shandilya branch = **Kulanayak** (senior ruling family)
- Ghosh (North Radhi) Shandilya branch = one of the 3 Kulin families

Both the Deva and Ghosh dynasties have Shandilya gotra lineages, but they are different families with different histories.

## North Radhi vs Bangaja: Key Differences

| Feature | North Radhi | Bangaja |
|---------|------------|---------|
| Center | Hooghly/Burdwan | Chandradwip (Barisal) |
| Founding | Ballalsen/Lakshmanssen (1070-1200 CE) | Danujamardana (1318 CE) |
| Kulin | Ghosh, Bose, Mitra (3 families) | None (Bangaja has no Kulin) |
| Maulika tiers | Siddha, Madhalya | Mahapatra, Siddha, Madhalya |
| Deva family rank | Siddha Maulika | Mahapatra |
| Robin's family | NOT North Radhi | **YES — Bangaja** (Mymensingh) |

## Book Contents Summary

This is the 3rd volume of the Uttarrariya (North Radhi) Kayastha series, covering 7 specific North Radhi Kayastha families with detailed genealogical tables and historical biographies. The volume documents specific prominent families from these 7 lineages during the colonial period (18th-19th century).
